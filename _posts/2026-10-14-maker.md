# October 2026: A Maker project with Mike McCaffrey

We have a special guest this month from the far north (Michigan): 
[Mike McCaffrey](https://www.pyohio.org/2026/program/speakers/mike-mccaffrey/)!

Mike wowed the attendees at PyOhio 2026 with his Maker project, a 
beautifully-crafted display of dynamic lights, much wiring, 
single-board computers, and of course Python.  He's continued working 
on the project and is bringing it to us so we can experience it 
personally, and learn how he did it.  He promises 

>     A hopefully entertaining overview of challenges faced and lessons learned during the first two years working on my first big maker project. With code snippets, photos and short demos, discuss problems overcome while developing new features and integrating new devices into my humble animated sign, which now includes color LED lights, new sound and input devices, various interactivity, and some surprises.
