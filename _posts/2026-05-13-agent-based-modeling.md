# May 2026: Agent-based modeling with MESA 

63 priests, 117 ministers, and 84 rabbis walk into a bar.
What happens next?  You'll never figure that out from first 
principles.  You're going to have to simulate the behavior 
of an individual - an "agent" - then put them together, let 
them interact, and see what happens.  That's the principle of
"agent-based modeling"; you can describe each "agent", but 
to find out how they will interact en masse, you simulate the 
situation and watch.

[Mesa](https://mesa.readthedocs.io/stable/) is a Python package 
to make agent-based modeling easy, attractive, and yes - it 
can be fun.
